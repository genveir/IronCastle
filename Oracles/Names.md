# Oracle: Names

Roll d100 once. Each roll gives three options: one cute, one cool, one sillier. Pick whichever fits. Printed across two pages in the rulebook as Names (1-50) and Names (51-00); combined here into one table.

Surnames are not needed. Iron Valley is a small enough community that no one uses them.
