# Oracle: Gender Presentation

Roll d100.

| Roll | Presentation |
|---|---|
| 1-25 | Feminine |
| 26-50 | Masculine |
| 51-75 | Androgynous |
| 76-00 | Other |

The book uses gender presentation rather than sex, because a person's biological sex may differ from the gender they present.

- **Feminine.** Having qualities or an appearance traditionally associated with women or girls.
- **Masculine.** Having qualities or an appearance traditionally associated with men or boys.
- **Androgynous.** Having qualities or an appearance somewhere between what is considered traditionally feminine and masculine.
- **Other.** Having qualities or an appearance unlike what is traditionally considered feminine, masculine or androgynous. This covers all modes of gender expression beyond the accustomed ones, and fantastical ancestries and species which do not follow the traditional human idea of gender presentation and are nonetheless valid.

In Iron Valley everyone respects each other's pronouns and gender presentation. People are free to be who they really are.
