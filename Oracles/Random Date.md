# Oracle: Random Date

Roll d100. A year is four seasons of 25 days each, so each roll maps to one day of the year.

| Roll | Date | Roll | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-25 | Spring 1 through Spring 25 | 51-75 | Fall 1 through Fall 25 |
| 26-50 | Summer 1 through Summer 25 | 76-00 | Winter 1 through Winter 25 |

Exact mapping: subtract the season offset from the roll to get the day. Spring is rolls 1 to 25, so roll 1 is Spring 1 and roll 25 is Spring 25. Summer is rolls 26 to 50, so roll 26 is Summer 1 and roll 50 is Summer 25. Fall is rolls 51 to 75, so roll 51 is Fall 1 and roll 75 is Fall 25. Winter is rolls 76 to 00, so roll 76 is Winter 1 and roll 00, read as 100, is Winter 25.

Used for Townie birthdays and any other date that needs generating. Put every birthday on the calendar. Gifts on a birthday have their value doubled.
