Note on tropical crops. The crop tables include bananas, coconut, durian, pineapple, lychee and similar. The game's premise is soil so fertile that anything grows anywhere, so these are intentional.

Flax is the key summer crop for the non-livestock alternatives: flax meal binds like eggs, and flax fibre becomes linen.
