# Bron

Player character. This document records the starting state at arrival. Live play state is handled separately.

## Identity

Deep dwarf of Kazad di Doron, under the Worldring Mountains. He/him.

Birthday: 24th of Spring. Arrived: 1st of Spring. Planning to stay: A long time.

## Stats

Edge 1, Heart 2, Iron 3, Shadow 1, Wits 2

## Skills

Mining, **Mechanic** (upgraded: +1 tick on hit), Art

All three carry +1 to rolls. Mechanic also has the first upgrade.

Satisfaction: 0 at start.

## Home

A former spotter's tower, remodelled at some point to be decorative, bought from Jerome, Baron of Iron, with the gold coin Darm gave him.

The ground floor sits at wall level, and everyone crossing that stretch of wall has right of way straight through it. There is no way around. Two cellar levels run inside the curtain wall, with a lower door opening out between the walls and down to the farmland ring. The top floor has big windows: the Worldring Mountains on the horizon one way, the whole castle laid out the other. The floors between are where he lives, and they have no furniture yet.

## Pack

A friendship bracelet. A song. One citrus fruit, uneaten.

## Backstory

Most deep dwarves never leave the tunnels under the Worldring Mountains. You can travel between all the great dwarven cities without once seeing the sky. Bron met an elf trader as a boy who told him about Iron Castle, and never stopped wanting to go.

His friends married and took steady jobs. One day his best friend Darm took him aside and made him promise to keep dreaming, and to one day make the dream real. Bron promised he would if he could. Darm pressed a gold coin into his hand, more money than Bron had earned in his life to that point, and said "then go when you're ready and you want to."

Bron stayed another year working up the nerve. Then he said his goodbyes, thanked Darm over drinks, took to the road, saw the sky for the first time, and walked until he reached the castle.

He spent the coin on the tower.

## Character notes

These are the engine of his arc. Keep them in mind when framing scenes and when Townies react to him.

**His skills are calibrated to the wrong audience.** Mining and Mechanic are baseline competence for a deep dwarf and remarkable to everyone else. At home he was unremarkable at the first and genuinely poor at the second, and he is insecure about it. In Iron Castle he is objectively the best mechanic in the place and he will not believe it.

**Art runs the other way.** Among dwarves he was a great artist. Here, surrounded by humans and elves, he is ordinary at the one thing he loves. He gave up his only real status to come here. He wants to get better.

**He is not fast and not sneaky.** Edge 1 and Shadow 1. He walked a continent rather than hurrying. Magic is not his.

**His house makes him social whether he likes it or not.** Everyone crossing that stretch of wall walks through his ground floor. A dwarf who took a year to work up the nerve to leave home now cannot avoid his neighbours. This is almost certainly what he needs.

**The coin is spent.** The promise to Darm is not behind him. He promised to make the dream real, and arriving was only the first box. The rest is whether he can actually live here.

**He has never farmed.** No Farming skill, no Creature Handling, no Cooking. He arrives genuinely unequipped for the life he walked a continent to reach and has to be taught it by neighbours if it's the life he wants.

**Only a few weeks in, it is his birthday.** He arrived on the first of spring. His birthday is the 24th. His first ever under an open sky, in a place where he knows almost nobody. Whether anyone notices is a story.

**Things he might want to build:** furniture for the empty floors. Every chair is a small vow kept.
