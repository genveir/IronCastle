## Birthdays

Birthdays are fully optional and the player will decide whether or not to engage. Remind them of birthdays at session start, but don't make anything out of the birthday unless the player explicitly tells you to. Townies will not mention it's their birthday, other people will not mention it, there is zero social pressure on the player to engage with the birthday.
